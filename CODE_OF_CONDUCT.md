# Code of Conduct

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, which is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part in Wanna Clip without harassment, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners
- Respect different opinions and ways of working
- Give feedback that is specific and constructive, and accept it graciously
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or spamming
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for explaining and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful. Maintainers will explain their reasons when they can.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer at **YOUR-EMAIL@example.com**. All reports are handled privately and taken seriously.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these steps when deciding the consequences of a violation:

1. **Correction.** A private note explaining what was wrong, and sometimes a request for a public apology. Used for unprofessional or unwelcome behavior.
2. **Warning.** A formal warning with consequences if the behavior continues, such as a temporary ban from interacting with the project.
3. **Temporary ban.** A ban from all interaction with the project for a set time, for serious or repeated violations.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression toward others.
